Privacy Notice and Consent

We collect and process your personal information for visitor registration, identity verification, facility access control, safety, security, and related administrative purposes.

Your information will be handled in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable organizational privacy policies. It will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill these purposes.

Read the full Privacy Notice

This registration may collect your name, contact details, organization or school, visit purpose, destination, schedule details, and related visitor information needed to process your facility visit.

The information may be used by authorized personnel for visitor management, access control, identity verification, safety, security, incident response, and administrative records related to your visit.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please coordinate with the Reception or Security Desk so your concern can be referred to the appropriate office.
